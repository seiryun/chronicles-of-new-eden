THE ARCHIVES

Fragments, records and histories from three capsuleer lives.

Origins & bloodlines

Achura — Agaroth Ethriael and Seiryun. Achura culture has remained distinct within the Caldari State. Both characters share this bloodline, but their personal paths diverge: one shaped by long military service, the other by spiritual cultivation.

Civire — Lenora. The Civire are described in EVE's character background as competitive and highly focused, able to remain composed under pressure in demanding industrial and military roles.

Historical records

The archive distinguishes between in-game records, official EVE background material and personal roleplay biographies. Dates, titles and memberships are shown only where supported by the supplied character records or player history.

Open archive

Additional screenshots, ships, medals, campaign memories and chronicles will be added as they are documented. This is a living archive, not a claim to completeness.
